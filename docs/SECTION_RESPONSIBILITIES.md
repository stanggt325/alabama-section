# What a USPSA Section is responsible for

Research brief, compiled 2026-09-14. This is the "why" behind the site map. Every page
on the site maps to an obligation in one of the three documents below.

## The three governing documents

| Document | Status | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **USPSA Amended & Restated Bylaws** (10 Mar 2026) | Corporate bylaws. §11.11 incorporates the Section policy as Exhibit G. §3.05 defines club membership. | https://storage.uspsa.org/bylaws/10MAR2026/USPSA%20-%20AR%20Bylaws.pdf |
| **Section Operations and Governance Policy** (Oct/Nov 2025) | The document that actually defines Sections, Section Coordinators, and their duties. Reviewed biennially by the Board. | https://s3.uspsa.io/bylaws/OCT2025/USPSA%20-%20Section%20Operations%20And%20Governance%20Policy.pdf |

## What a Section is

A state or geographic area with at least two affiliated clubs, chartered by USPSA
(Policy §1.1). It is "the local link to USPSA for the members and Section clubs"
(§1.2), providing:

- direct support and communication to affiliated clubs
- local coordination of activities and competitions
- distribution of National Championship slots
- member services and organizational representation

Sections cannot cross Area boundaries without a hardship petition (§2.4). Alabama is
wholly inside Area 6 (AL, FL, GA, MS, NC, SC, TN; Area Director William Herrington).

## The Section Coordinator

**Elected** by the member clubs, one vote per member or per club as the Section bylaws
choose (§3.1(a)). Alabama: one vote per club (AL Bylaws Art. 5, 7.D). An election must
be held **at least every two years** (§3.1(b)); Alabama's bylaws set a **one-year term,
1 April to 31 March** (Art. 7.D). It is the SC's and the clubs' joint responsibility to
run the election (§3.1(d)). A majority of club presidents can call a special election at
any time (§3.5(b)).

**Qualifications** (§3.2): current annual USPSA membership and NROI Range Officer
(Level I) or higher. Losing either vacates the office.

**Removal** (§3.4): the Area Director may remove for cause; appealable to the USPSA
Board by the SC or any club in the Section. **Vacancy** (§3.5): the Area Director
appoints an interim SC until an election.

### Duties (Policy §4.1, verbatim headings)

| | Duty | Site page |
|---|---|---|
| a | **Promotion** of USPSA, its policies, rules, ideals | whole site |
| b | **Communication**: keep the Area Director and national office informed of Section activity | `/notices/` is the public record |
| c | **Championship support**: promote the Area Championship and Nationals | `/championship/`, `/nationals-slots/`, `/calendar/` |
| d | **Results and fees**: ensure every club's match results and activity fees reach USPSA within **30 days** of the match | `/resources/` (club officer checklist) |
| e | **Club affiliation**: recommend clubs for affiliation/disaffiliation to the Area Director | `/clubs/` |
| f | **Club visitation**: visit each club at least once a year during a match | `/calendar/` standing dates |
| g | **New club development** | `/clubs/` "Starting a club" |

Plus, from the "Responsibilities" section of the policy: conduct elections per the
bylaws, provide financial accounting to member clubs, maintain membership and NROI cert.

### Alabama-specific duties (AL Bylaws)

- Jurisdiction over USPSA rules/policy matters in the Section and over Nationals slot
  allocation (Art. 7.A). Executive Committee has jurisdiction over everything else
  (7.B). Neither over a club's internal affairs (7.C).
- Chair the Executive Committee (Art. 4.A).
- Recommend new clubs for Section membership; assist unaffiliated clubs (Art. 3).
- Review the bylaws for currency whenever club representation changes (Art. 12).
- Complimentary entry into all Section club matches including the Championship (7.D).

## Bylaws obligations

- A Section cannot be chartered without bylaws approved by the Area Director and the
  Board Chair (§2.5(a)). Bylaws are subordinate to USPSA Bylaws and rules (§2.5(b)).
- Bylaws **must** set out a Nationals slot distribution method that always considers
  match placement, may consider club membership and event assistance, and **never**
  uses a lottery or random selection (§2.5(c)-(d)).
- After **any amendment**, send bylaws to the Area Director and the national office
  (§2.6(a)). Provide current bylaws **at every SC election** (§2.6(b)).
- If bylaws are not provided to the national office annually, USPSA's default standards
  apply (§2.6(c)). Failing to maintain current bylaws for **two consecutive years** is
  grounds for **dissolution** (§8.1(b)).

### Known conflict to fix

AL Bylaws Art. 11.A: "IF additional slots remain for any division, the Section
Coordinator shall conduct a **random drawing** for the slots." This contradicts Policy
§2.5(d) ("Slots may never be awarded by lot"). The next amendment must replace the
drawing with a placement-based fallback (e.g., next finisher in the division at the
Section Championship, then by overall placement, then by a qualifier-series average as
Eastern Colorado does). The site's `/nationals-slots/` page already flags this.

Other gaps in the 2019 bylaws worth closing in the same amendment:

- No notice period for meetings or amendments (NE Section uses 14 days for meetings and
  15 days for amendments; ECO uses 14 days with a published agenda).
- No Treasurer or Webmaster role, no requirement that the SC be the primary holder of
  a Section bank account, no handover procedure beyond "by 15 January".
- Signatory table (Art. 12) is frozen at Feb 2019 and lists clubs that no longer exist
  (AL19 HTF, AL21 2d Amendment, and AL22/AL23 later).
- No mention of Steel Challenge clubs.

## Meetings and notifications

The policy itself does not prescribe notice periods; it delegates to Section bylaws.
What must be communicated, and to whom:

| What | To whom | When | Authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Executive Committee meeting (annual) | Club presidents/representatives | At least once a year, during/after the Championship | AL Art. 6 |
| Special meetings | Committee members | Called by SC or any two members; may be electronic | AL Art. 6.A, 6.C |
| SC election | Member clubs | Before 31 March each year (AL); at least every 2 years (USPSA) | AL 7.D; Policy 3.1 |
| Election result + current bylaws | Area Director, national office | At each election | Policy 2.6(b) |
| Amended bylaws | Area Director, national office | After any amendment | Policy 2.6(a) |
| Annual accounting of Section funds | Member clubs | Annually | Policy 5.3; AL 7.E |
| Section activity | Area Director, national office | Ongoing | Policy 4.1(b) |
| Club affiliation/disaffiliation recommendations | Area Director | As needed | Policy 4.1(e) |
| Nationals slot awards | Slot winners, then USPSA | After the Championship, before USPSA's registration deadline | AL Art. 11 |
| Match results + activity fees | USPSA | Within 30 days of each match (club duty; SC ensures) | USPSA Bylaws 3.05(d); Policy 4.1(d) |
| Dissolution warning response | Area Director | 90 days to remedy | Policy 8.2 |

The `notices` collection on the site has a category for each of these. Templates for
election and meeting notices are in `src/content/notices/_TEMPLATE-*.md.txt`.

## Money

- Sections may levy up to **$1.00 per contestant** per sanctioned match, but only if
  their bylaws provide for it and ensure accountability to clubs (§5.1). Alabama's
  bylaws instead charge a flat **$20 per club per year, due before 30 January**
  (Art. 7.E).
- Funds cover the SC's office and projects benefiting all clubs. **Not** travel, meals,
  lodging, or match fees (Art. 7.E; §5.2).
- **Full accounting to the clubs annually** (§5.3). Funds transfer to a new SC by
  15 January (Art. 7.E).

## Section Championship

- The Executive Committee must see that one is held annually and acts as its steering
  committee: host club, Match Director, policies, host agreement (AL Art. 10.A.1).
- Host keeps proceeds after activity fees and expenses; some returned as prizes
  (10.A.3). Host provides trophies (10.A.2).
- Section Champion titles per recognized division, Alabama residents only (10.A.4).
- 2026: Talladega Practical Shooters (Talladega Marksmanship Park), match book at
  https://alstateuspsa.com. 2027: Talladega again, 29 Apr - 1 May 2027 (USPSA calendar).

## Nationals slots

- USPSA awards activity-based slots to each Section from pooled club activity (AL
  Art. 9; USPSA Nationals Slot Policy: roughly one slot per 10 participants per
  division, capped). Performance-earned slots (prior Nationals, Area Championship) go
  directly to shooters.
- Section distributes by placement at the Section Championship, division champions
  first (Art. 11.A). Leftover-slot drawing is now prohibited (see above).
- Unused slots go back to USPSA (ECO 10.4 is a good model for the mechanics).

## Dissolution risk (Policy §8)

A Section can be dissolved for: fewer than 2 clubs; no current bylaws on file for 2
years; failing to hold required elections; SC vacant 12+ months; or Board discretion.
90 days to cure after notice. Alabama's exposure is the bylaws-on-file item, given the
site was down and the last known revision is 2019.
