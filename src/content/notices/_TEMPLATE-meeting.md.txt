Per the Alabama Section bylaws (Art. 6), the Executive Committee meets at least once a year, during or immediately after the Section Championship.

**When:** [date, time]
**Where:** [location]
**Quorum:** a majority of committee members (Art. 6.B)

**Agenda**

1. Roll call of member clubs
2. Section Coordinator report
3. Annual accounting of Section funds (Policy §5.3; Bylaws Art. 7.E)
4. Section Championship: host club and dates for next year (Art. 10.A)
5. Nationals slot distribution (Art. 11)
6. Bylaws amendments, if any
7. Club business
8. Election of Section Coordinator (if an election year)
