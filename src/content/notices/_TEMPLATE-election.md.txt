Per USPSA's Section Operations and Governance Policy §3.1, Sections must elect a Section Coordinator at least every two years. Per the Alabama Section bylaws (Art. 7.D) the term runs 1 April to 31 March and the vote is one per member club.

**When:** [date, time]
**Where:** [location, or "by electronic means" per Art. 6.C]
**Who votes:** the president of each member club, or their designated representative (Art. 8)

**Nominations.** Send nominations to the Section Coordinator at [email] by [date]. Candidates must hold current USPSA membership and an NROI Range Officer (Level I) or higher certification (Policy §3.2).

**Bylaws.** The Section must provide current bylaws to the Area Director and the national office at the time of each election (Policy §2.6(b)). The current bylaws are at /governance/bylaws/.
