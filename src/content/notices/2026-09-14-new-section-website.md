The Alabama Section's previous website went offline in 2025. This site replaces it.

What lives here:

- **Notices.** Official Section notifications: Executive Committee meetings, Section Coordinator elections, bylaws amendments, Nationals slot distribution, and club affiliation changes. If the Section is required to tell you something, it will be posted here.
- **Clubs.** Every USPSA-affiliated club in the Section, with links to their websites and PractiScore pages.
- **Section Championship.** Past results back to 2010 and the current match.
- **Governance.** The Executive Committee, the current bylaws, meeting minutes, and the election schedule.

Club officers: if your club's listing is out of date, [contact the Section Coordinator](/contact/).
